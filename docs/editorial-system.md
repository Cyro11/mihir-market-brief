# Editorial System

## Product Thesis

Mihir Market Brief is a visual finance newsletter for banking judgment. It should read like a concise institutional desk note, not a generic news digest.

The newsletter uses free-access sources, but the editorial filter should resemble paid finance media:

- prioritize stories that serious market participants are already watching
- explain why the story matters for valuation, financing, deals, sectors, or company strategy
- separate fact, sourced professional commentary, and our own interpretation
- use charts and visuals to make the market setup legible quickly

## Core Sections

### What Matters

Five to seven high-impact headlines. Each headline must answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter for banking, markets, or investing?
- What is the likely first-order effect?
- What is the likely second-order effect?

Primary filters:

- rates, Fed, inflation, labor, growth
- credit and financing conditions
- M&A, IPOs, restructuring, activist situations
- AI capex and major company strategy shifts
- sector-level read-throughs for banks, software, industrials, consumer, healthcare, fintech
- policy and regulation with deal or valuation impact

### Deal Watch

Track one transaction or situation per issue.

Required fields:

- target / buyer / seller / sponsor
- transaction type
- announced or rumored terms
- strategic rationale
- valuation context
- financing or capital structure angle
- regulatory risk
- what changed since last issue
- what to watch next

### Macro Desk

This is not a data dump. It translates macro into finance consequences.

Examples:

- higher rates -> higher WACC -> tighter LBO math -> fewer sponsor exits
- sticky inflation -> fewer cuts -> refinancing pressure -> sector dispersion
- strong labor market -> resilient consumer -> delayed Fed cuts -> mixed equity multiple impact

### Markets Snapshot

Use visuals for:

- equity indices
- Treasury curve
- credit spreads
- oil / dollar / gold where relevant
- sector pulse

Every chart needs one sentence explaining the signal.

### Company Lens

Rotating company or sector note. It should connect current news to:

- business model quality
- competitive position
- margin structure
- valuation sensitivity
- M&A relevance

### IB Learning Corner

One practical banking concept tied to live news:

- accretion / dilution
- LBO debt capacity
- valuation multiples
- DCF discount rates
- IPO window dynamics
- refinancing risk
- restructuring waterfall basics

## Source Rules

Preferred free sources:

- Reuters, AP, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, MarketWatch
- FRED, BLS, BEA, Treasury, Federal Reserve
- SEC filings, company press releases, investor presentations
- earnings call transcripts and public executive commentary

Validation sources:

- Wall Street Journal
- Financial Times
- New York Times

When accessible, use WSJ / FT / NYT to validate that a theme is important. Do not rely on them as the only source if the content is paywalled.

## Quality Bar

Each item should include:

- fact pattern
- source trail
- professional quote or commentary where available
- first-order impact
- second-order impact
- banking relevance
- confidence level when interpretation is uncertain
